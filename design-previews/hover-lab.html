Hover lab

Three ways to do the two-line hover, all cut from the same wedge motif as the card ribbons. Hover each control. The fields also respond to keyboard focus — press Tab to check that path, since a hover-only effect leaves keyboard users with nothing.

A — Sweep

Two slivers cross the box and exit. They are deliberately not parallel: the front sliver is steeper, so the pair opens apart as it travels. Most noticeable of the three — it reads as a highlight passing over the control.

Enters from the right and stays for as long as the pointer is inside, then retreats. The wedge is widest along the bottom edge, so the diagonal leans back and the mass sits low — the same way the bottom ribbon on the card pivots low and wide.
